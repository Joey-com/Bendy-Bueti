How this works
  • Take the level test first. It puts you in the right stage and shows your weak spots.
  • Alternate Day A and Day B, 5–6 days a week. The app remembers which one is next.
  • Every day ends with a splits block: front splits on Day A, middle splits and pancake on Day B.
  • Retest every 4 weeks. Move up when the test says so, not by the calendar.
  • Contract-relax: in a split, push down into the floor for 5 seconds, relax, then sink a little lower. Repeat through the hold.
  • Tension is fine. Sharp, pinching or joint pain means back off.
  • Gear: M10 for face pulls, Roman chair for Nordics, two yoga blocks or thick books for splits, a towel or sliders for split slides.
